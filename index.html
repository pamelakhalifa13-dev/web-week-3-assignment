How to use this tracker

Welcome to My Budget Tracker! This website helps you keep track of your daily expenses.

  1. Enter the name of the expense.
  2. Enter the amount spent in Kenyan Shillings.
  3. Select the appropriate expense category.
  4. Click the Add Expense button.
  5. View your recorded expenses in the expenses table.

Note: The form is currently a visual demonstration. Adding and saving expenses will be implemented in a future version.
